# A8 – The runner wallet and stake consent by signed message (D-A8-1)

## What changed
- **Runner wallet.** All plain cells of the runner's own secp256k1 lock (the service key's `blake160`) are the wallet. `csr identity`, `csr wallet status` and the dashboard show its address. The operator sends about 700 CKB there once (the registry cell uses about 540 CKB).
- **The runner owns and pays its registration** (dual-lock args `runner ‖ runner`) and signs every transaction with the service key. `csr register remove` returns the registry cell's CKB to the wallet.
- **Tips go into the runner wallet** (`payout_lock_hash` = the wallet's lock hash); the job lock is unchanged.
- **Stake consent.** The DAO deposit stays in the operator's wallet; its owner signs the one-line text `CSR-STAKE-CONSENT-v1|…` ("Sign message" in Neuron). `csr-registry` verifies the signature (errors 21–23). See `spec/registry.md`.
- **Automatic sweep.** At `sweep_threshold_ckb` (default 1,500) the runner sends `sweep_amount_ckb` (default 1,000) to `sweep_address`.
- **Dashboard.** Page "Wallet & registration" (`#/wallet`), Overview card "Wallet", sweep settings. No browser dialogs; the removal is confirmed in the page.
- **Removed:** payout cell and `csr payout …`, `[payout] lock_args`, `csr register prepare`, `/api/payout…`, `/api/registry/prepare-create`, `/api/tx/submit-signed` and their dashboard controls.
