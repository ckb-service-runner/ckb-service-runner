# A7 – Stake as a referenced Nervos DAO deposit, payout cell (D-A7-1)

## What changed
- **Registry v2** (`spec/registry.md`): `payout_lock_hash` (the old float slot) and a new last field `stake` (out point). `csr-registry` checks on creation (and on an owner update that changes stake or owner) that the stake is a Nervos DAO deposit of ≥ 10,000 CKB owned by the registry owner (errors 18–21). "Still deposited" and "one deposit, one registration" are checked by the CSR Network Monitor.
- **Float removed**: `csr-float-lock`, the float cell, `csr float …`, `/api/float…`. Tips go to the operator's **payout cell**, a standard anyone-can-pay cell (`[payout] lock_args`). `csr-job-lock` checks that the tip arrives at `payout_lock_hash`.
- **Runner**: `csr payout status|prepare-create|prepare-withdraw`, `csr register prepare … --stake`, `csr register attest --stake`, keeper pays into the payout cell. `csr-dev`: `dao-deposit`, ACP deployment.
- **Dashboard**: page "Stake & payout", Overview card "Payout", stake of the CSR monitor in "My rank".
- No slashing and no job collateral in CSR.

## Demo (devnet)
See "Operator flow on devnet" in `docs/testing.md`: `csr-dev dao-deposit --ckb 10000`, `csr payout prepare-create`, `csr register prepare --attestation att.json --owner-lock-args … --stake 0x<tx>:<index>`; then open `http://127.0.0.1:8080/#/payout` (dashboard bind as configured) to see payout balance and stake status.
Tests: `node --test --test-concurrency=1 acceptance/a7/p1.test.mjs … p5.test.mjs`.

## Migration
Old (v1) registrations are not migrated: they expire with their attestation. Operators create a payout cell, make a DAO deposit, attest with `--stake` and register again. `deployments/testnet.json` keeps the old registry/job deployment until the manual redeploy phase.
